Our Story

More Than Dining. An Experience.

In the heart of Downtown Dubai, AURELIA brings together fire, flavour and finesse. Every plate leaves our kitchen as a small piece of theatre — seasonal ingredients, honest technique, and presentation worthy of the occasion.

From slow-braised lamb to charcoal-grilled seafood, our kitchen honours the classics of Middle Eastern hospitality while embracing the polish of international fine dining. The room is calm, the service is warm, and every table is set like it matters — because it does.
